BEFORE I TOUCH IT

Android Private Beta

For invited testers only. This is a test-signed build installed outside Google Play.

Download

The Android beta APK is being prepared. The download button will appear here once the tested build is published.

What to expect

Android may ask you to allow your browser or file manager to install apps from outside Google Play. Play Protect may also show a warning because this is a private sideloaded beta.

You can turn the “Install unknown apps” permission back off immediately after installation.

Test the real workflow

  1. Launch the app and start a job.
  2. Allow camera access and capture at least three clearly different real photos.
  3. Add a note and mark one step important.
  4. Finish documenting and confirm the Home cover and Job thumbnails show the real photos.
  5. Force-close and reopen the app. Confirm the job, photos, note, important marker, and order are still present.
  6. Open Reassembly and confirm it starts with the last captured step and walks backward.
  7. Hide and restore the reassembly controls. Confirm you stay on the same photo and step.
  8. Use Android system Back from Capture, Job review, Appearance, Privacy, and Reassembly. Note anything surprising or any data loss.
  9. Try System, Light, and Dark appearance modes.

Send your results

Please include your phone manufacturer/model, Android version, whether installation succeeded, whether all real photos rendered correctly, whether data survived force-close/relaunch, whether reverse reassembly was correct, and whether Android system Back behaved normally.

Do not send private photo content unless it is necessary to explain a problem.
