BEFORE I TOUCH IT

Privacy Policy

Provider: Reasonmade
Contact: support@reasonmadesoftware.com
Effective date: October 1, 2026

Summary

Before I Touch It stores the jobs you create locally on your device. Your job content is not sent to Reasonmade.

Information stored on your device

Before I Touch It stores information you choose to create in the app, including:

  • job names and status;
  • captured step photos;
  • step notes;
  • important-step markers;
  • step order and timestamps;
  • related app data needed to display and manage your jobs.

Information sent to Reasonmade

Your job photos, notes, job names, and step history are not sent to Reasonmade.

Before I Touch It does not use an account, analytics service, advertising SDK, tracking service, cloud-sync service, or Reasonmade-operated backend in the current release.

Camera access

Before I Touch It requests camera access so you can take the photos you choose to add to a job.

Captured photos are stored with the app on your device.

Secure handling

Job content is stored in the app's private local storage using storage provided by the device operating system. Reasonmade does not operate a server that receives or stores your job photos, notes, job names, or step history.

Security of the device itself, including device passcodes, operating-system security, and any device-level backup or restore features, is controlled by the device platform and your device settings.

Sharing and selling

Reasonmade does not sell your job data or share your locally stored job content with third parties through the app.

Retention and deletion

Your job data stays on your device until you delete a job or remove the app.

When you delete a job, Before I Touch It removes its job and step records and removes its stored photos from the app's local storage.

Deleted local job data cannot be restored by Reasonmade.

Support

If you contact Reasonmade for support, Reasonmade receives the information you choose to include in your message or attachments. Support correspondence may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, maintain support records, protect the service, or satisfy legal obligations.

You may ask Reasonmade to delete support correspondence that can reasonably be associated with your request by contacting the address below, subject to any information that must be retained for legal, security, or recordkeeping reasons.

Changes to this policy

If these privacy practices change, Reasonmade will update this policy and its effective date.
